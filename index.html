How I work

User Research

I pick the method based on what the team needs to decide, whether that's interviews, a survey, usability testing, or a card sort. Then I share the findings in a form product and engineering can act on.

UX & Interaction Design

Information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and prototypes. I design so people move through complex systems without much effort, working to WCAG standards so interfaces stay usable across devices.
