How I work

Research first, AI throughout

User Research

Interviews, surveys, usability testing, and card sorting, matched to what the project needs. Good research is the difference between solving the real problem and a guessed one.

UX & Interaction Design

Information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and prototypes. I design so people move through complex systems without much effort, working to WCAG standards so interfaces stay usable across devices.

AI-Assisted Workflow

I use Claude and other AI tools for research synthesis, content development, and design exploration. On the Polly Banerjee project, an 8-site competitive audit that would have taken days took a few hours.

Project & Client Leadership

Scoping work, running timelines, and translating between what clients ask for and what the team builds. I have led projects solo and inside cross-functional teams.
