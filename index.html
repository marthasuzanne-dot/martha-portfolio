How I work

Research first, AI throughout

User Research

Interviews, surveys, usability testing, card sorting. The method depends on the project, but the point is always to find out what people actually do rather than what they say they do.

UX & Interaction Design

Information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and prototypes. I design so people can find their way through complex systems without much effort.

AI-Assisted Workflow

I use Claude and other AI tools for research synthesis, content development, and design exploration. On the Polly Banerjee project, an 8-site competitive audit that would have taken days took a few hours.

Design Systems & Accessibility

Reusable patterns and WCAG accessibility practices, so interfaces stay consistent and usable across devices and audiences.
