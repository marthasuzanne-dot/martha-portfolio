How I work

Research first

I pick the method based on what the team needs to decide, whether that's interviews, a survey, usability testing or a card sort. Then I share what I learned in a form product and engineering can act on.

Calm, clear design

My background is in psychology, so I design around how people read, scan and decide. I keep screens quiet, labels plain and the next step easy to find.

Design ready to build

Information architecture, flows and high-fidelity design, handed off in Figma with components, specs and notes for engineering. On small teams I often run the project too.

AI in my process

I use Claude for research synthesis, planning and checking my work, and Claude Code to build and refine designs. When I design AI features, a person reviews what the AI drafts before anything saves.
